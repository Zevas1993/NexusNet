## USPTO Filing Items To Prepare

1. Provisional application cover sheet or Application Data Sheet identifying the application as provisional.
2. Names of all inventors.
3. Residence of each inventor.
4. Title of the invention.
5. Correspondence address or customer number for USPTO communications.
6. Attorney or agent name and registration number, if applicable.
7. Docket number, if applicable.
8. Any U.S. Government agency with a property interest in the application, if applicable.
9. Filing fee under the current USPTO fee schedule and the correct entity status, if claimed.

## Package Review Before Upload

1. Confirm the specification begins with `Title`, not internal status language.
2. Confirm the specification has no code, repository paths, private workstation paths, drafting notes, or legal strategy notes.
3. Confirm the five figures are included as actual black-and-white drawings.
4. Confirm the title in the cover sheet or ADS matches the specification title exactly.
5. Confirm all inventor names and residences are correct.
6. Confirm the correspondence address is complete.
7. Confirm the current fee immediately before filing because USPTO fees can change.
