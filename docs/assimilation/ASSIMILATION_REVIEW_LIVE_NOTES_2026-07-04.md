### Recursive Dreaming / Dreamer / Dream Reviewer

- Accepted posture: recursive dreaming is first-class and continuous, not only event-triggered.
- Native output: `Recursive Dreaming And Imagination Foundry`.
- The Dreamer is intentionally high-temperature, high-variance, and not grounded in current reality.
- The Dreamer's purpose is to generate ideas that may violate current assumptions, combine unusual domains, mutate failures, and propose candidate improvements.
- The Dreamer must not be punished for being ungrounded; only for failing to yield useful or testable hypotheses after review.
- The Dream Reviewer is separate, low-temperature, grounded, conservative, and evidence-seeking.
- The reviewer converts dream outputs into testable hypotheses, sandbox experiments, eval fixtures, expert-birth proposals, merge/split proposals, or archived dead ends.
- During downtime/sleep, the Dreamer should continuously dream from historical NexusNet events.
- Historical inputs include failed tasks, near misses, slow runtime paths, expert disagreements, rejected proposals, user corrections, eval regressions, source-health changes, tool failures, teacher gaps, workflow traces, and promotion/rollback receipts.
- Dreaming should aim to improve every and any aspect of NexusNet: experts, AOs, routing, memory, runtime, skills, tools, UI, teacher selection, evals, policies, and curricula.
- Live stuck-problem dreaming and downtime/sleep dreaming are both required lanes.
- Full loop:
  `historical event stream or stuck signal -> high-temp Dreamer -> dream candidates -> low-temp Dream Reviewer -> testable hypotheses -> sandbox/eval/world-model simulation -> evidence packet -> promotion tribunal -> mother-brain approval`
- Dreams never directly mutate production.

### Cluster 9 Minimum Multi-Teacher Birth Verification

- Accepted rule: every Expert, Assistant Orchestrator, Orchestrator, and core node needs two or more teachers for the distillation and verification process.
- Multiple teachers are required because a single-teacher lane is not legitimate enough for expert birth, AO birth, orchestrator birth, merge, split, or permanent promotion.
- The teacher panel is a minimum evidence contract, not a static roster. A node can have more than two teachers when risk, domain complexity, disagreement, or performance variance requires it.
- Each node must be judged against its own teacher panel and baseline, not against a global teacher or another node's teacher.
- A valid teacher panel should include at least two meaningfully different teacher roles, such as:
  - a domain generator or domain specialist;
  - a verifier, critic, evaluator, simulator, retrieval/citation teacher, safety reviewer, runtime teacher, or compact apprentice.
- For high-risk or high-authority nodes, two teachers is only the floor. Medical, legal, finance, crypto, security, mental health, holistic/integrative medicine, robotics, autonomy, governance, and self-modification lanes need stronger panels and human/governance gates.
- Full verification before birth must capture teacher disagreement, teacher strengths, teacher failures, child-vs-teacher scorecards, eval evidence, distillation rights, provenance, rollback, and archive-not-delete retirement state.
- No teacher, teacher panel, or teacher council replaces the mother brain. The mother brain owns teacher selection, evidence review, promotion, rollback, merge/split decisions, retirement, and final authority.
