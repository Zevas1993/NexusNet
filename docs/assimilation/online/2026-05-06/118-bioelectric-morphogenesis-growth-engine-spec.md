## Finding

Developmental biology shows that form can emerge from local cell behaviors coordinated by higher-level patterning signals. Levin's bioelectricity work is especially relevant as a computational metaphor: stable target morphology, collective intelligence across scales, and repair/regeneration through control signals rather than micromanaging every part.

## NexusNet Assimilation Target

Create a computational growth engine for NexusNet. Instead of hand-wiring every future capability, define target morphology, local growth rules, viability constraints, and regeneration/repair behavior for software components.

## Proposed NexusNet Components

- `TargetMorphology`: desired system shape such as cortex, memory, sensory, motor, policy, and replay regions.
- `GrowthRule`: when to split, merge, specialize, prune, inhibit, or reinforce a component.
- `BioelectricSignalAnalog`: abstract control signal for salience, inhibition, repair, novelty, and differentiation.
- `RegenerationRoutine`: rebuilds damaged or missing indexes, routes, memories, or eval reports from evidence.
- `MorphologyAudit`: compares current NexusNet structure to target shape and reports malformed growth.
