## Finding

The missing piece is not a single model, ancient claim, or consciousness-uploading technique. It is a governed developmental cortex: a system that keeps a live self-model, learns causal world models, grows modular reference-frame circuits, explores through intrinsic motivation, dreams/simulates before acting, consolidates during sleep, preserves diverse evolutionary stepping stones, and only promotes changes through evidence gates.

## NexusNet Assimilation Target

Make NexusNet a developmental intelligence substrate rather than a static AI app. The system should grow from a seed architecture through staged, measurable, reversible development, while the operator keeps authority and the evidence ledger keeps memory honest.

## Proposed NexusNet Components

- `DevelopmentalCortexKernel`: coordinates self-model, world model, reference frames, memory, policy, and growth loops.
- `SeedGenome`: initial safe architecture, invariants, model roster, memory schema, tool caps, and growth permissions.
- `CorticalNursery`: sandboxed curriculum worlds for staged learning and capability growth.
- `DreamingSimulator`: imagination and deterministic simulation loop for candidate futures.
- `SleepConsolidator`: replay, abstraction, pruning, and memory/circuit proposal generation.
- `GrowthArchive`: GFlowNet/open-ended archive of candidate circuits and improvements.
- `CausalInterventionLab`: sandboxed tests that separate association from causal mechanism.
- `ViabilityAndAuthorityGate`: homeostasis, privacy, safety, operator override, and promotion tribunal.

## Promotion Gates

- Keep all growth shadow-only until eval, policy, provenance, and operator review pass.
- Require source-linked evidence for every memory, circuit, and route change.
- Use staged maturity levels: seed, infant sandbox, adolescent tool use, adult governed operation.
- Define objective maturity tests for each stage.
- Preserve rollback, lineage, and stop conditions for every developmental transition.
- Keep consciousness and life claims out of product language.

## Risks

- A developmental architecture can become too complex without narrow first milestones.
- Poorly designed intrinsic rewards can produce reward hacking or useless novelty.
- Self-model and world-model errors can compound unless causal tests and reality-gap checks are routine.
- Governance must be built before growth is given authority.
