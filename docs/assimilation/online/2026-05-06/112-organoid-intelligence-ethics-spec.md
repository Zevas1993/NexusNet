# Organoid Intelligence Ethics Spec

Status: P2 online assimilation target. Research-only. No wetlab protocols or biological experimentation instructions.

## Source Evidence

- Organoid intelligence Frontiers lead article: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/science/articles/10.3389/fsci.2023.1017235/full
- Baltimore declaration editorial: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/science/articles/10.3389/fsci.2023.1068159/full
- FinalSpark Neuroplatform: https://finalspark.com/neuroplatform/
- DishBrain Neuron paper page: https://www.cell.com/neuron/fulltext/S0896-6273(22)00806-6
- Source status: peer-reviewed article pages and official platform page.

## Finding

Organoid intelligence and DishBrain-style closed-loop cultures show an early research path for living neural substrates interacting with digital environments. The field is ethically sensitive and technically immature. NexusNet should not become a wetware project, but it can learn from closed-loop embodied feedback, sparse stimulation, plasticity, and explicit ethics gates.

## NexusNet Assimilation Target

Create an organoid-inspired closed-loop learning specification for artificial systems only. The transfer target is not human tissue; it is a digital growth loop where a model receives structured stimulation, acts in an environment, changes internal routing, and is monitored for emergent capability and risk.

## Proposed NexusNet Components

- `ClosedLoopStimulationProtocol`: digital task stimulus, feedback, reward/surprise, and safety boundary.
- `PlasticityObservationTrace`: what changed after stimulation and whether it improved behavior.
- `EthicsBoundaryRecord`: states that biological substrates are out of scope unless a qualified external ethics process exists.
- `EmergentCapabilityMonitor`: watches for new behaviors, unsafe shortcuts, or reward hacking.
- `EmbodiedLearningSandbox`: isolated environment for developmental experiments.

## Promotion Gates

- No biological protocols, procurement, culture methods, or wetlab execution instructions in NexusNet.
- Keep all near-term implementation digital/simulated.
- Require ethics review before any human-derived biological data or substrate integration.
- Record stimulation, response, and plasticity evidence in traces.
- Treat organoid claims as early science, not production readiness.

## Risks

- Wetware topics can drift into unethical or unqualified experimentation.
- Closed-loop learning can reward unsafe shortcuts if feedback is poorly designed.
- Biological intelligence analogies can be overread as consciousness claims.
